## Cross-phase conclusions

- No Banking77 test row is a pristine release test. The exact roles and taint status are in [`data_lineage.json`](data_lineage.json).
- The served research architecture is the base decision head: packed Choice through 19 candidates, then the learned K=8 shortlist and packed rerank at 20+.
- Phase 9 exists and is reproducible, but it is not served.
- Ordinal Score is implemented but **RESEARCH ONLY**. Served Score remains categorical.
- Multilingual checkpoints can run, but their release calibration is not validated; all multilingual primitives are **RESEARCH ONLY**.
- Automation is disabled. No 1%, 2%, 5%, or 10% production risk guarantee is supported.

## Blind evaluation integrity

The first frozen CLINC150 `test` process scored all 4,500 rows but failed during final optional RSS collection before aggregate persistence. It was not rerun and is permanently **DEVELOPMENT-EXPOSED**; see `artifacts/release/blind_clinc150_test_failed_run.json`.

The untouched CLINC150 `val` partition was then frozen as the replacement blind set. It was evaluated once with aggregate-only output. The sealed result is `artifacts/release/blind_clinc150_val_result.json`.
