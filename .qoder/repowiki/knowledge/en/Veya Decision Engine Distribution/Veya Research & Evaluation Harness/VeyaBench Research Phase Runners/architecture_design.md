Each `run_phaseN_*.py` is a self-contained entry point for one research experiment; there is no central launcher — the parent directory acts as a flat registry of phase scripts that all import from two shared layers: `protocol.py` (SplitLedger enforcing train/calibration/validation/test disjointness, recall@k, hard-negative mining, permutation/risk-coverage metrics) and `rivals/` (adapters for NLI, sentence-transformers, Jev/TypeSafe used by every phase that benchmarks against external classifiers). Phases are ordered chronologically (3→5 exploratory, 6→7 reranker/adaptation, 8→9 semantics/hypothesis, 10→11 domain retriever/noise, 12 context audit), with later phases consuming artifacts produced by earlier ones (e.g. Phase-5 choice model frozen before Phase-6 reranker training; Phase-10 retrievers feed Phase-11 noise ablations; Phase-12 audits build on Phase-10/11 outputs). The `__init__.py` declares the module's purpose as the held-out evaluation protocol and synthetic-row harness.
