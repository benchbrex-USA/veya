Two-layer layout: (1) data/protocol primitives — `phase10_data.py` builds a contamination-aware split of Banking77 train + SGD train (with a frozen DEVELOPMENT_SERVICES set) plus CLINC150 train as schema-held-out validation, emitting JSONL splits, `candidates.json`, a `registry.json`, and a `splits.json` freeze under `artifacts/experiments/phase10`; `phase11_noise.py` provides a fixed `CONDITIONS` enum of label-preserving perturbations (short/long prefix/suffix, middle insertion, boilerplate wrapper, JSON noise, repeated low info) selected deterministically via SHA-256 on row id; `phase11_protocol.py` exposes `tokenization_audit` and `mine_hard_negatives_with_lineage` which screen normalized-label duplicates and base-embedding near-duplicates above a semantic-similarity threshold, producing an auditable lineage with `records_sha256`. (2) runner scripts — `run_phase10_train.py` is the base harness that loads a frozen ModernBERT-large release bundle, caches 1024-d encoder embeddings in `frozen_embeddings.npz`, trains `ProjectionPair` heads (shared/dual, then hard-negative refresh), and selects among B2/B3/B4 variants against CLINC150. `run_phase11_train.py` reuses `_embedding_texts`, `load_inputs`, `model_scores`, `retrieval_metrics`, `EPISODE_CARDINALITIES`, and `LEARNING_RATE` from the Phase-10 harness, adds a clean→noisy curriculum with optional invariance loss (`INVARIANCE_WEIGHT=0.05`), runs the Phase-11 protocol's hard-negative mining, and writes `phase11_noise_robust_k8.pt`. `run_phase11b_backbone_capacity.py` mirrors Phase-11 but varies output dimension to 256/512 by zero-padding/truncating the Phase-10 projection weights. Supporting runners `run_phase10_blind_massive.py`, `run_phase10_diagnostics.py`, `run_phase10_validate.py`, `run_phase11_train.py`, `run_phase11_validate.py` consume the same artifacts without opening consumed blind sets.
