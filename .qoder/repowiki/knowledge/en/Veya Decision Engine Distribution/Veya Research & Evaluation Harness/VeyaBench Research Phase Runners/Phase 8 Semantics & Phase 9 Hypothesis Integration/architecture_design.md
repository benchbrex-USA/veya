Two cooperating stages under `research/veyabench`:

- Phase 8 (`build_phase8_semantics.py`, `run_phase8_eval.py`): a data-generation script that derives `candidate_semantics.json` from the PolyAI categories list and the Phase-6 confusion edges, then an evaluation harness that scores three definition levels (D0 label-only, D1 with one training utterance, D2 adding a contrastive sibling) against the frozen Phase-6 shortlist using `raw_choice_logits` / `score_sets`. Results are gated by bootstrap CI on conditional accuracy and high-confidence error counts.
- Phase 9 (`run_phase9_hypothesis.py`, `run_phase9_integrate.py`): an independent hypothesis-scoring pipeline built on `veya.experimental.hypothesis.HypothesisScorer` + `CompatibilityHead`. It freezes the production checkpoint via `write_control()`, builds a corpus of natural top-8 and forced-positive groups, runs permutation/security/paraphrase gates before training, optimizes either a fresh head or a suffix of encoder layers with AdamW, evaluates with `score_groups`, fits temperature via `veya.calibration.fit_temperature`, and only then opens the release test rows `test:1300:3080` once. `run_phase9_integrate.py` is the post-deploy re-checker that loads `artifacts/release/manifest-phase9.json`, verifies fingerprint logits, permutation stability, temperature refit, HTTP routing, security, latency, and AG News regression.

Dependency direction is strictly downward into shared infrastructure: `phase6_protocol` (split indices, bootstrap), `run_phase3_eval` (data loaders, ROOT), `run_release_eval` (manifest loading), and `veya.*` (release bundle, server, calibration, experimental hypothesis). The two phases share no code; they communicate through JSON artifacts in `artifacts/experiments/{phase8,phase9}`.
