Each `run_*.py` script is self-contained and guarded against re-runs (e.g. `run_blind_clinc150.py` refuses if the result file exists and verifies SHA-256 hashes of the freeze manifest, dataset, and harness itself). The Jev rival requires both `TYPESAFE_API_KEY` set and `typesafe_sdk` installed; otherwise it reports unavailable rather than fabricating results. `write_baseline.py` writes to `research/results/post_fix_baseline.json` after loading the production tokenizer from `models/production/tokenizer`.
