Each file is executed directly as `python run_phaseN_*.py`; they set `USE_TF=0` and `TOKENIZERS_PARALLELISM=false` at import time, require the prebuilt production checkpoint at `<repo>/models/production/model.safetensors`, and write results to `<repo>/artifacts/experiments/phaseN/`.
