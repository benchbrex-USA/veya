Two parallel, code-free trees:
- `research/configs/` contains small JSON manifests describing experiments. Each manifest declares a `name`, `status` (e.g. `SCAFFOLDED`, `IMPLEMENTED_NOT_RESEARCH_VALIDATED`), an optional `train` command pointing at a launcher module under `research.configs.launch_*`, hardware requirements (`dev` / `full`), expected artifacts, and sometimes `compare_against` / `metrics`. They are consumed as data by research runners rather than imported as Python.
- `research/data/` is a flat collection of dataset directories, one per benchmark (`ag_news`, `banking77`, `clinc150`, `hwu64`, `massive`, `phase10`, `sgd`). Formats vary by source: HuggingFace parquet (`ag_news`, `massive`), PolyAI CSV splits (`banking77`), JSONL dialogues with per-split `schema.json` (`sgd`), and custom JSON/JSONL task definitions (`phase10`, `hwu64`). A top-level `registry.json` catalogs datasets with provenance fields (`source`, `license`, `version`, `domain`, `task_type`, `split_role`, `known_contamination_risk`) so consumers can reason about contamination and licensing without parsing raw files.
Dependency direction is one-way: research code reads these JSON/CSV/parquet files; nothing in this scope imports from the rest of the repo.
