Checkpoints are pulled lazily from Hugging Face Hub under `veyadynamics/veya` (with optional `subfolder=` like `multilingual` or `typed-decisions`); authentication goes through `HF_TOKEN` env var or the `token=` parameter. The default English checkpoint is ~421M params, multilingual ~322M; running all three simultaneously requires ~1.16B parameters. GPU memory fallback automatically drops to CPU on OOM, printing guidance for newer NVIDIA architectures.
