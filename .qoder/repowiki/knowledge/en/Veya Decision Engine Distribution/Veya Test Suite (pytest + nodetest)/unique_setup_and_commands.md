End-to-end scripts require real artifacts and must be invoked directly, not via pytest: `python3 tests/test_local_e2e.py [model_root]` (defaults to `~/veya_models`, expecting `veya/`, `veya-multilingual/`, `veya-typed-decisions/`) and `python3 tests/test_model_server_e2e.py [repo_root]` (needs `artifacts/release/manifest.json` and `models/production`). The `VEYA_DEVICE` env var selects device (default `cpu`).
